JAMB 2027: Why Starting in October Beats Starting in January

Most candidates only panic when registration opens. Here is a simple 14-week plan that puts you months ahead before the crowd even begins.

Michael Joseph
·📅 18 October 2026·⏱ 2 min read

The Early Start Advantage

Every year, thousands of candidates wake up in January, see registration open, and realise they have not touched a past question. By then the exam is only weeks away. If you start today, you have a head start that most of your competitors will not have.

The UTME is not a test of genius. It is a test of familiarity: with the syllabus, with the question styles, and with the clock. Familiarity comes from repetition, and repetition needs time.

A 14-Week Plan That Works

Weeks 1 to 4: go through the JAMB syllabus for each of your four subjects and mark every topic as green (I know it), yellow (I half know it) or red (I do not know it). Be honest. Weeks 5 to 10: attack the reds first, one topic per day, then do 10 past questions on that topic. Weeks 11 to 14: switch to full timed practice, a 40-question block at a time.

Make It Stick

Study in short, focused sessions of 25 to 40 minutes. Close the book and test yourself instead of re-reading.
