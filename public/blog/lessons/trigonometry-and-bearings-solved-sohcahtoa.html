Trigonometry and Bearings: SOHCAHTOA and Bearing Problems Solved

Use SOHCAHTOA to find sides and angles in right-angled triangles, then apply it to elevation and bearing problems with every step shown.

SOHCAHTOA in a right-angled triangle

In a right-angled triangle, the longest side is the hypotenuse. The other two sides are named from the point of view of the angle you are working with: the opposite side is across from the angle, and the adjacent side is next to it.

SOHCAHTOAsin = Opp/Hyp ; cos = Adj/Hyp ; tan = Opp/AdjLabel the sides from the angle you are using, not from the right angle

Pick the ratio by asking which two sides are involved: the one you know and the one you want. If they are Opp and Hyp, use sin. If they are Adj and Hyp, use cos. If they are Opp and Adj, use tan.

Finding a side

QuestionIn a right-angled triangle the hypotenuse is 13 cm and one angle is 40 degrees. Find the side opposite the 40 degree angle.
  1. Known side is the hypotenuse and wanted side is opposite so use sin
  2. sin 40 = x / 13
  3. x = 13 x sin 40
  4. sin 40 = 0.6428 so x = 13 x 0.6428 = 8.36
AnswerThe opposite side is 8.36 cm (2 decimal places)

To find the adjacent side instead, use cos: 13 x cos 40 = 13 x 0.7660 = 9.96 cm. As a check, 8.36 squared plus 9.96 squared is about 169, which is 13 squared. Correct.

Finding an angle

Use the inverse function. A ladder 5 m long leans against a wall and reaches 4 m up. The angle with the ground has Opp = 4 and Hyp = 5, so sin A = 4/5 = 0.8 and A = sin-1(0.8) = 53.13 degrees. This is the famous 3, 4, 5 triangle, where the other side is 3 m.

Angles of elevation and depression

The angle of elevation is measured upward from the horizontal. The angle of depression is measured downward from the horizontal. The angle of elevation from A to B equals the angle of depression from B to A.

QuestionFrom a point 40 m from the foot of a tower, the angle of elevation of the top is 35 degrees. Find the height of the tower.
  1. The ground distance is adjacent and the height is opposite so use tan
  2. tan 35 = h / 40
  3. h = 40 x tan 35
  4. tan 35 = 0.7002 so h = 28.01
AnswerThe tower is about 28.0 m high

Bearings

A bearing is the direction of one point from another. Three rules never change:

  1. Measure from North.
  2. Measure clockwise.
  3. Write three figures: 045, 090, 270.

Due East is 090, due South is 180, due West is 270. To find a back bearing, add 180 if the bearing is under 180, otherwise subtract 180. So if B is on a bearing 065 from A, then A is on a bearing 245 from B. If C is on 300 from D, then D is on 120 from C.

1Draw a North line at each point
2Mark the angle clockwise
3Join the points
4Spot the right angle
5Use SOHCAHTOA or Pythagoras

A full bearing problem

QuestionA ship sails 20 km due East and then 15 km due North. Find how far it is from the start and the bearing of the start from its final position.
  1. East and North meet at a right angle so use Pythagoras
  2. Distance = sqrt(202 + 152) = sqrt(625) = 25 km
  3. Bearing of the final point from start: tan A = 20/15 so A = 53.13 degrees giving 053.1
  4. Back bearing = 53.1 + 180 = 233.1
AnswerIt is 25 km from the start and the start lies on a bearing of 233.1 degrees

Draw a sketch every time. Most mistakes happen because the diagram is missing or the angle is measured from the wrong direction.

Frequently asked questions

What does SOHCAHTOA mean?

Sin = Opposite / Hypotenuse, Cos = Adjacent / Hypotenuse, Tan = Opposite / Adjacent.

How is a bearing measured?

From North, clockwise, as a three-figure angle such as 045 or 230 degrees.

How do I find a back bearing?

If the bearing is less than 180 degrees, add 180. If it is 180 or more, subtract 180.

What is an angle of elevation?

It is the angle above the horizontal line when you look up at an object.
