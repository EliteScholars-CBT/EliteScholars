Mean, Median and Mode (Including Grouped Data) Solved Step by Step

Find the mean, median and mode from a list and from a grouped frequency table, with every step shown and checked.

The three averages in one minute

  • Mean = sum of all values / number of values.
  • Median = the middle value after arranging in order.
  • Mode = the value that appears most often.

Each one answers a different question. The mean uses all the data but is pulled by extreme values. The median is not affected by extreme values. The mode shows what is most common, and a set of data can have no mode, one mode or more than one.

Raw data example

Ten students scored: 5, 8, 6, 9, 7, 8, 3, 8, 4, 10.

QuestionFind the mean, median and mode of 5, 8, 6, 9, 7, 8, 3, 8, 4, 10.
  1. Sum = 5 + 8 + 6 + 9 + 7 + 8 + 3 + 8 + 4 + 10 = 68
  2. Mean = 68 / 10 = 6.8
  3. Arrange: 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 8, 8, 9, 10
  4. Median is the average of the 5th and 6th values = (7 + 8)/2 = 7.5
  5. Mode = 8 because it appears three times
AnswerMean = 6.8, median = 7.5, mode = 8

Note that you must order the data before finding the median. Many students lose marks by picking the middle value of the unordered list.

Grouped data: the mean

When data are in classes, use the midpoint of each class. Here are marks of 30 students.

ClassFrequency fMidpoint xfx
1 - 1045.522
11 - 20615.593
21 - 301025.5255
31 - 40835.5284
41 - 50245.591
Mean of grouped dataMean = (sum of fx) / (sum of f)x is the class midpoint and f the frequency
QuestionUse the table to find the mean.
  1. Sum of f = 4 + 6 + 10 + 8 + 2 = 30
  2. Sum of fx = 22 + 93 + 255 + 284 + 91 = 745
  3. Mean = 745 / 30 = 24.83
AnswerMean = 24.83 marks (to 2 decimal places)

Grouped data: the median

Use the cumulative frequency (running total). The cumulative frequencies here are 4, 10, 20, 28, 30. Half of 30 is 15, so the median lies in the class where the running total passes 15, which is 21 - 30.

Median of grouped dataMedian = L + ((N/2 - F) / f) x cL lower class boundary, F cumulative frequency before the class, f class frequency, c class width
QuestionFind the median of the grouped marks.
  1. Median class is 21 - 30 so L = 20.5
  2. N/2 = 15 and F = 10
  3. f = 10 and c = 10
  4. Median = 20.5 + ((15 - 10)/10) x 10 = 20.5 + 5 = 25.5
AnswerMedian = 25.5

Grouped data: the mode

The modal class is the class with the highest frequency, here 21 - 30 (frequency 10). To estimate the mode:

Mode of grouped dataMode = L + (d1 / (d1 + d2)) x cd1 is frequency minus the one before it, d2 is frequency minus the one after it

Here L = 20.5, d1 = 10 - 6 = 4, d2 = 10 - 8 = 2 and c = 10. So mode = 20.5 + (4/6) x 10 = 20.5 + 6.67 = 27.17 (2 decimal places).

Mistakes to avoid

  • Using class limits instead of boundaries in the median and mode formulas. Use 20.5, not 21.
  • Dividing the sum of fx by the number of classes instead of the sum of f.
  • Using the frequency as the mode. The mode is the value, not the count.
  • Forgetting the cumulative frequency must be BEFORE the median class.
  • For the mean from a list, dividing by the wrong count.

A quick sanity check is that the mean must lie between the smallest and largest value, and for grouped data the answers should fall inside the sensible class.

What to do today

  • Find all three averages for a list of ten numbers
  • Rebuild the grouped table and compute the mean
  • Compute the cumulative frequency and median
  • Calculate the mode with the d1 d2 formula
  • Underline the class boundaries in your notes

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between mean, median and mode?

Mean is the sum divided by the count, median is the middle value when data is ordered, and mode is the most frequent value.

How do I find the median of an even number of values?

Order the values and take the average of the two middle ones.

Why use class midpoints for grouped data?

Individual values are not known, so the midpoint is used to represent every value in the class.

What are class boundaries?

They are the limits that remove gaps between classes. For a class 21 to 30, the boundaries are 20.5 and 30.5.
