HOW THE RULE WORKS
  Inactive = no partner login AND no click on their link for 14 days -> yellow in admin + warning email + bell notice.
  A week later, if still inactive (21+ days total) -> suspended automatically, suspension email sent, bell notice.
  Any login or link click in between clears the warning. Admin "Reactivate" restarts the clock.
